# Part 15 · Verbal Aptitude

Official GATE 2027 wording: "Verbal aptitude: basic English grammar (tenses, articles, adjectives, prepositions, conjunctions, verb-noun agreement, and other parts of speech); basic vocabulary (words, idioms, and phrases in context); reading and comprehension; narrative sequencing."

Anytime track: no dates, never in the day's plan, practised only when the student asks (AGENTS.md 2.10). Built from zero on 19 Nov 2026, from every GATE paper's verbal PYQs (all branches, 2010–2026). About 3–4 of the 15 GA marks in the CS papers, 1–2 questions a set since 2023.
Order: verbs and tenses, agreement and the small words, comparison and word forms, then vocabulary, sentence completion and analogies, then reading and inference (the biggest share since 2014), then ordering sentences.

## VA01 · Verbs and tenses
notes: general-aptitude/01-verbal
hours: 1.5

### Tenses
- VA01.1 T The tense is fixed by the rest of the sentence: a time word (yesterday, since, by next year), the other clause, or the kind of statement (a habit or general truth takes the simple present; an action interrupted takes the past continuous).
- VA01.2 Q Fill the verb form the context demands. PYQ: CE2026s2-ga1, ME2026-ga1, DA2025-ga2, EE2025-ga1, CE2017s1-ga1, CH2017-56, CE2014s2-ga1, EC2014s1-ga1, ME2014s1-ga1, CH2013-57
- VA01.3 T Reported speech and past narratives shift back one tense (is → was, has → had, will → would); an indirect question keeps statement order ("asked where he was").
- VA01.4 Q Verbs after said, asked, returned; indirect questions. PYQ: EE2023-ga1, 2017s1-ga1, EE2017s1-ga1, 2014s2-ga2, EE2014s2-ga2

### Conditionals and verb patterns
- VA01.5 T Real conditions: if + present, will + verb ("if it rains, we will stay"); after if, when, once, the future is said in the present.
- VA01.6 Q Real and future conditions, and modals of possibility. PYQ: CE2023s1-ga1, ME2019s2-ga1, EC2014s2-ga2, ME2014s2-ga2
- VA01.7 T Unreal conditions: if + past, would + verb (present unreal); if + had + participle, would have + participle (past unreal); inverted: "Had I known…", "Were I…".
- VA01.8 Q Unreal and inverted conditionals. PYQ: EC2025-ga2, ME2019s1-ga2, CE2017s2-ga1, EC2017s1-ga2, CE2016s2-ga1, EC2015s3-ga1, ME2015s2-ga1, 2014s3-ga2, EE2014s3-ga2, 2013-59, ME2013-57, EC2012-57, EE2012-58
- VA01.9 T Verb patterns: some verbs take -ing (avoid, enjoy, mind), some to + verb (decide, hope, want), some a bare verb (make, let, help); prepositions take -ing.
- VA01.10 Q Gerund, infinitive or bare verb after a verb or preposition; causatives. PYQ: ME2023-ga1, CE2022s2-ga1, EE2020-ga2, EC2013-59, EE2013-58, 2011-58
- VA01.11 Q Past forms and participles of irregular verbs. PYQ: EC2021-ga5, CE2018s2-ga1, CH2016-ga2, ME2016s2-ga2

## VA02 · Agreement and the small words

### Subject and verb
- VA02.1 T The verb agrees with its real subject: "along with", "as well as", "including" do not make a subject plural; "the number of" is singular, "a number of" plural; each, every, neither are singular.

### Prepositions, articles, connectors, pronouns
- VA02.4 T Fixed prepositions belong to the word before them (comply with, adept at, averse to, independent of); learn them as pairs.
- VA02.5 Q Prepositions fixed by a verb, adjective or noun. PYQ: DA2026-ga6, EE2024-ga6, CE2020s2-ga1, CH2020-ga1, EC2020-ga1, ME2020s2-ga1, EE2019-ga2
- VA02.6 T Time and place: at a point (at dawn), on a day or surface, in a period or space; for a duration, since a starting point; between two, among many; beside (next to) vs besides (in addition).

- VA02.8 T Articles: a before a consonant sound, an before a vowel sound (an hour, a university); the for something specific or unique; no article for general plurals and uncountables.
- VA02.9 Q Choose a, an, the or nothing. PYQ: CE2026s2-ga6, ME2026-ga6, 2025s1-ga1, CE2019s2-ga5, 2016s2-ga1, EE2016s1-ga1, 2015s3-ga2, CE2015s1-ga3, 2013-60, ME2013-59
